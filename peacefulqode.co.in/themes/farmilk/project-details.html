Frequently asked question

Find answers to common questions about our dairy farm, cow care, and how we produce fresh, high-quality milk with complete hygiene and sustainable practices in mind.

01
How do you care for your cows’ welfare?

Cow welfare is central to our farm. They have space, clean water, nutritious food, and regular veterinary care.

02
How long does fresh milk stay good?

When properly refrigerated, our milk stays fresh for several days. Keep it below 4°C (39°F) and consume by the “use by” date.

03
How do you ensure milk safety?

Milk safety is our top priority. We follow strict hygiene and daily quality checks to ensure fresh, clean, and nutritious milk.

04
Do you offer organic milk options?

All our milk is naturally produced, following sustainable practices. Some farms may offer organic milk; check with us or your retailer.
